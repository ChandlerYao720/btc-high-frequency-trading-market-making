06 / SYSTEM

Training supervision ends before the live-time decision path begins.

The system separates historical labels and fitting from frozen scoring, inventory-aware quote sizing, constrained execution, and sequential account attribution.

Current implemented system, balanced overview. Ten nodes are retained from the full mechanism map; low-level storage, model serialization, and diagnostic branches are collapsed. The accent marks the quote-to-fill handoff.
